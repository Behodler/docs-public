# Phoenix Ecosystem Overview

**phUSD is the core product of Phoenix.**

It is the central asset around which the whole ecosystem is built. When phUSD is minted, protocol-owned capital increases and Phoenix is permanently strengthened.

Ultimately, this is the most important way to grow the protocol:

**more phUSD minted → more capital captured → more yield created → a stronger Phoenix.**

The price of phUSD is shaped by the interaction between **phUSD staking** and the **Balancer liquidity pool**.

All the other features are designed to accelerate and support that core loop.

They do this by:

- Boosting the phUSD price
- Increasing liquidity
- Growing the Yield Funnel
- Increasing the yield premium
- Supporting Nudge Rewards
- Creating stronger incentives to mint, stake, and use phUSD

Through these mechanisms, and especially through the growth of the yield premium, **phUSD staking can become one of the most attractive above-market DeFi yield venues — paid in USDC.**

That is the core Phoenix USP:

**phUSD as an evergreen DeFi asset.**

An asset that can grow through market volatility.

An asset that can offer above-market yield because volatility strengthens the system rather than breaking it.

In other words:

**An anti-fragile DeFi asset.**

Remember: **phUSD price and phUSD staking yield have an inverse relationship.**

When the price of phUSD falls, phUSD staking APY rises, attracting demand and helping pull price back toward its target.

When the price of phUSD rises above its target, arbitrageurs are incentivised to mint and sell phUSD, returning the price toward target while increasing protocol-owned capital and strengthening the yield premium.

So when asking what is best for the ecosystem, the answer is not really one feature in isolation.

The answer is the **interplay between all of them.**

Every part of Phoenix is designed to reinforce every other part.

That means users do not need to feel like they are being selfish by chasing the highest yield or choosing the option that best suits their own risk profile.

There is no part of Phoenix that is simply a drag on the rest of the system.

If users actively try to maximise their own returns, that is fine. Phoenix is designed so that user activity feeds back into the wider protocol.

**Do what is best for you — but understand the tradeoffs.**

## Main Options and Tradeoffs

| Option | User benefit | How it helps Phoenix | Main tradeoffs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Stable Staker** | High current APY on supported assets such as USDC, USDe, and DOLA. Easier exit because users can unstake without selling phUSD into a pool. | Grows vault TVL, expands the Yield Funnel, boosts phUSD staking APY, and helps fund Nudge Rewards. This indirectly supports LSP NFT minting. | APY falls as more capital enters. It may feel lower-friction, but it is not automatically lower-risk because funds are deployed into vaults and strategies. |
| **phUSD Staking** | Earn USDC yield from staking phUSD. Useful for users who want yield paid in USDC rather than a reward token. | Creates direct demand for phUSD, supports price, encourages minting, grows protocol-owned capital, and strengthens the yield premium. | Users need to hold phUSD. Exiting depends on phUSD market price and available liquidity. APY depends on Yield Funnel size and total participation. |
| **Liquidity NFT Staking** | Mint Liquidity NFTs with USDS and stake them to earn phUSD. Current staking rate is around 30% APY. | Creates protocol-owned liquidity and helps tilt the phUSD price upward, which can trigger mint-and-sell arbitrage and increase phUSD supply. | Exit friction is higher. NFTs can be sold, used to claim from the Yield Funnel, or kept staked, but exits are less immediate than standard staking. |
| **Whale Mint Incentives** | Earn USDC rewards for batch-minting 40 Liquidity NFTs. Rewards can be boosted by Ratchet NFTs. | Creates large liquidity events, increases protocol-owned liquidity, and helps force the minting cycle forward. | Requires larger capital commitment. Exit friction is tied to the NFT position and reward timing. |
| **Ratchet NFTs** | Minted with USDC and staked to earn phUSD. Current staking APY is around 45%. | Funds Whale Mint Incentives and supports the reward reservoir that pushes LSP NFT minting forward. | Less liquid than standard staking. Users rely on staking yield, future Yield Funnel claims, or NFT resale demand. |

## How the Pieces Strengthen Each Other

Stable Staker grows the Yield Funnel.

The Yield Funnel boosts phUSD staking APY.

Higher phUSD staking APY attracts more demand for phUSD.

More phUSD demand encourages more minting.

More minting increases protocol-owned capital.

More protocol-owned capital creates more yield.

More yield strengthens the whole ecosystem.

At the same time, the Yield Funnel also supports Nudge Rewards, which help accelerate LSP NFT minting.

LSP NFT minting increases protocol-owned liquidity, tilts the phUSD price upward, and creates more opportunities for minting arbitrage.

That arbitrage creates more phUSD supply, more protocol-owned capital, and a stronger yield premium.

So the system is not a set of isolated farms competing against one another.

It is one connected loop.

## What Should Users Consider?

If you want the easiest exit, **Stable Staker** may be attractive because you can unstake without needing to sell phUSD into a pool.

If you want yield paid in USDC and are comfortable holding phUSD, **phUSD staking** may be the cleanest option.

If you want steadier NFT-based APY and are comfortable with higher exit friction, **Liquidity NFT staking** or **Ratchet NFTs** may be attractive.

If you want to take a more active role in strengthening the protocol, **Whale Mint Incentives** and **Liquidity NFTs** are especially powerful because they create protocol-owned liquidity and help drive forced minting.

There is no single answer that is best for everyone.

Each option has strengths.

Each option supports Phoenix in a different way.

The important thing is that Phoenix is designed so that user incentives and protocol incentives point in the same direction.

You can stake supported assets.

You can stake phUSD.

You can mint and stake NFTs.

You can use NFTs to claim from the Yield Funnel.

You can simply hold ecosystem tokens and let Phoenix activity work in the background.

All of these actions support the same larger loop:

**more liquidity → more yield → more phUSD demand → more minting → more protocol-owned capital → more yield.**

The bigger Phoenix gets, the bigger the Yield Funnel becomes.

The bigger the Yield Funnel becomes, the stronger phUSD staking becomes.

The stronger phUSD staking becomes, the more attractive phUSD becomes.

And the more attractive phUSD becomes, the stronger Phoenix becomes.

**Phoenix grows stronger together.**
